Musly is a modern, privacy-focused client for Subsonic-compatible servers (Navidrome, Subsonic, Airsonic, Gonic), Jellyfin, and local audio files.

<b>Features:</b>
• <b>Multi-Provider Support:</b> Connect to Navidrome, Subsonic, Jellyfin, or play local files directly on your device.
• <b>Smart Mixes & Recommendations:</b> 100% on-device taste-learning algorithm providing personalized mixes ("Made For You", "Listen Again", "Top Hits").
• <b>Offline Downloads:</b> Batch download your favorites, full albums, or artist discographies with auto-resume.
• <b>Android Auto:</b> Full Android Auto support with responsive browsing tree and hardware controls.
• <b>Multi-Server Profiles:</b> Switch between multiple server accounts seamlessly with automatic Dual LAN/WAN latency fallback.
• <b>Synced Lyrics:</b> Time-synced lyrics with LRCLIB fallback and customizable display styles.
• <b>Privacy-First:</b> 100% telemetry-free, zero analytics, and hardware-backed credential storage.
• <b>Modern Design:</b> Fluid animations, dynamic album art color palettes, and adaptive light/dark themes.
